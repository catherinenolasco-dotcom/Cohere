People Operations built from zero

Ridecell | Dec 2018 - Jan 2026

Challenge

On day one there was no documentation beyond outdated files, headcount lived in spreadsheets, and employee questions had no tracked channel.

What I did

Built a 20+ procedure SOP library with owners and backup coverage, launched ASKPeople on Jira and Confluence with tiered SLAs, and made Sapling the HR single source of truth, integrated with ADP and Zapier connections.

Result

Scaled from 3 to 5 countries, passed 6 consecutive clean SOC 2 Type 2 audits, cut approval cycles from 1-2 days to 2-3 hours, and resolved 5-10 tickets a day in 24-48 hours.

What I would do differently

I'd have started the SOP library and ticketing system in the first months. Documenting as I went would have been easier than catching up later.

One approval path for every HRIS change

Prezent AI | Feb 2026 - Apr 2026

Challenge

HRIS changes ran through an undocumented, unapproved process, which created audit risk. Some people pushed back that a form was too process-heavy.

What I did

Built a Claude-powered Employee Action Form that requires documentation and approval before any HRIS change, plus Notion New Hire and Offboarding workflows with automated Slack and email reminders.

Result

100% adoption within one to two months, an audit trail that had not existed before, and preboarding within 24 hours of offer acceptance.

What I would do differently

I'd have piloted the form with one team first. Some people said it was too process-heavy, and early proof from a small group might have won them over faster.

A reorganization with cost visibility

Prezent AI | Feb 2026 - Apr 2026

Challenge

A reorganization impacting 18 employees across the US and India needed severance, COBRA and total cost numbers, and leadership had never seen RIF cost before.

What I did

Built AI-enabled severance and cost modeling with Claude and Gemini that calculated severance, COBRA cost and COBRA months owed and drafted the agreements, tracked on a shared spreadsheet for Payroll and IT.

Result

First-time RIF cost visibility, and agreement prep cut from 20 minutes per employee to about 3 hours total.

What I would do differently

I'd have built the cost model before the reorganization was decided. Leaders could then have compared scenarios rather than just seeing the cost of the chosen one.

My first 90 days and beyond

A proposed plan built around the success picture in the role description.

Days 1 to 30

Listen and map

  • Meet the HR Operations team, Finance, IT and the Rippling implementation group.
  • Inventory every headcount, attrition and people cost report and where each number comes from.
  • Write down who uses each metric and what they think it means today.
Days 31 to 60

Design the first moves

  • Reconcile People and Finance headcount for one month-end and log every gap.
  • Draft the metric dictionary and employee data model definitions with the HRIS team.
  • Add validation checks and an approval path for changes, like the Employee Action Form.
Days 61 to 90

Ship and measure

  • Publish the first executive and board-ready headcount, attrition and people cost report from Rippling.
  • Automate the recurring report and give managers a self-service view.
  • Document the process in an SOP with audit evidence Finance and IT can review.
By month 6

The outcome

  • One agreed set of definitions, reconciled between People and Finance every month.
  • Recurring reports automated, with fewer manual data requests.
  • Audit-ready reporting that scales as the company grows.

This is a starting point. I would adjust it after the first conversations with the HR Operations team, Finance and IT.
